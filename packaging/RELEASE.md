FloatingDock 1.6.1 restores the top bar's original appearance.

- Restore the neutral translucent top-bar tint from 1.5.10 and its original blur strength.
- Remove the blue tint and increased opacity introduced on the top bar in 1.6.0.
- Keep the search, menu, window-animation and tray improvements from 1.6.0.

### Downloads

- **FloatingDock-Setup-x64.exe** — per-user installer.
- **FloatingDock-Portable-x64.zip** — extract the entire archive and run FloatingDock.exe.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** — SHA-256 hashes of both downloads.

.NET and Windows App SDK are included. Windows 10 1809+ / Windows 11 x64; unsigned builds. Existing settings, pinned apps and personal icons are preserved.

### По-русски

Верхней панели возвращены прежние нейтральная прозрачность и сила размытия из версии 1.5.10. Убраны синий оттенок и повышенная непрозрачность, появившиеся в 1.6.0. Доработки поиска, меню, анимаций окон и трея сохранены.
